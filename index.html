Reference panel

Typical healthy ranges

These are general adult reference points, not personal medical targets.

  • Blood pressureBelow 120 / 80
  • BMI18.5 - 24.9
  • Resting heart rate60 - 100 bpm
  • Total cholesterolBelow 200 mg/dL
  • Fasting glucose70 - 99 mg/dL

What the report checks

The score is an educational screening estimate based on patterns in the supplied patient fields.

  • Age and body-mass index
  • Systolic and diastolic pressure
  • Cholesterol and glucose categories
  • Smoking, alcohol and activity

01 / Early awareness

Small signals deserve attention.

Blood pressure, body composition, activity, smoking and metabolic markers can work together. A screening view makes those patterns easier to discuss with a clinician.

02 / Better conversations

Bring a clearer picture to the appointment.

Use this report as a starting point for questions, not as a substitute for an examination, diagnostic test or emergency care.
